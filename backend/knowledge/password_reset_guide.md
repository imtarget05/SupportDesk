# Password Reset & Account Recovery

## 1. Self-Service Password Reset
Click 'Forgot Password' on the login screen. A reset link will be sent to your registered email address within 2 minutes.
The reset link expires after 15 minutes and can only be used once.

## 2. Email Not Received
Check your spam or junk folder. Ensure emails from noreply@supportdesk.internal are allowlisted.
