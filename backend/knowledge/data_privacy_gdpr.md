# Data Privacy & GDPR Compliance

## 1. Right to Access & Data Portability
Under GDPR and CCPA, customers can export all account data in machine-readable JSON format via Settings > Privacy > Export Data.

## 2. Right to Erasure
To request complete deletion of your personal data, submit a ticket under category 'Privacy & Compliance'.
Data will be purged from active databases and backups within 30 days.
