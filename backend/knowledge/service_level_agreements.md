# Service Level Agreements (SLA) Matrix

## 1. Response Times by Priority
- Urgent: First response within 1 hour, resolution within 4 hours.
- High: First response within 4 hours, resolution within 8 hours.
- Normal: First response within 8 hours, resolution within 24 hours.
- Low: First response within 24 hours, resolution within 48 hours.

## 2. Proactive Warning Triggers
An automated alert is triggered when 80% of the SLA duration has elapsed without resolution.
