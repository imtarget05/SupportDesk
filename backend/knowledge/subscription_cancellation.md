# Subscription Cancellation Guide

## 1. How to Cancel
To cancel your subscription, navigate to Settings > Billing > Manage Subscription and click 'Cancel Subscription'.
Your account will remain active until the end of the current billing cycle.

## 2. Data Retention after Cancellation
Your data is securely archived for 30 days after subscription termination before permanent deletion.
You can reactivate your account at any time within this 30-day window.
