# Billing & Refunds Policy

## 1. 14-Day Refund Guarantee
Customers are eligible for a full refund within 14 days of initial subscription purchase if service expectations are not met.
No questions asked during the 14-day window.

## 2. Refund Processing Time
Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 3 to 5 business days depending on the card issuer.
Bank transfers may take up to 7 business days.

## 3. Non-Refundable Items
Setup fees, domain registrations, and customized professional services are strictly non-refundable.
